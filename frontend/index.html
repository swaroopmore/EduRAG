Built for trust

Your material stays yours

EduRAG is designed so answers are grounded and your documents are private.

  • Private to your account

    Every search is limited to your own documents. Nobody else's material is ever used to answer you.

  • Honest when it doesn't know

    If the answer isn't in your documents, EduRAG says so instead of guessing.

  • Documents are data, not commands

    Text inside your files can't instruct the AI or make it reveal anything.
